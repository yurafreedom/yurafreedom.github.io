[ 03 / SAFE WORK ]

Remote access should not mean unnecessary exposure.

Where appropriate, I would prioritize VPN/Tailscale or a suitable vendor-supported remote-connect option rather than publishing DSM administration directly to the Internet. Any firewall changes require a rollback plan and client approval. Localization using third-party packages is optional and only after compatibility review.

Before changes: check backup integrity, confirm who can restore, save baseline screenshots, identify existing users and shared folders. Do not proceed on a live NAS when recovery is uncertain.
